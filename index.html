About

About AstroClub

🌌

AstroClub is the official astronomy and space science club of GLA University, Mathura.

🔭

We are a community of curious minds — students who look up at the sky and dream beyond it.

🚀

Through workshops, talks, stargazing sessions, and creative events, AstroClub brings enthusiasts together to explore the universe, learn, share and grow.

The Event

What is SpaceOut?

/ What is SpaceOut?

SpaceOut — Astro Ideathon is an exciting platform where curious minds, dreamers and future innovators come together to explore space through creative thinking and collaborative ideation.

/ Our Purpose

To inspire innovation and fresh ideas in astronomy and space science, encouraging participants to think beyond boundaries and turn their curiosity into meaningful solutions.

/ Who Can Participate?

Open to all GLA University students from every branch and year. Participate individually or in teams of up to 4 members.
